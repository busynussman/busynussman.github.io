Privacy Policy

Bonsai Ma is designed to be private. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

Bonsai Ma does not require an account and does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking technology, crash-reporting services, or third-party software development kits. The app does not send data to Mark McClusky or any third party.

Data stored on your Mac

The app stores a small amount of preference and daily-tree state locally on your Mac so it can remember settings such as Always on Top and maintain the current day’s artwork. This information stays on the device and is not used to identify you.

Networking and cloud services

The App Store version of Bonsai Ma does not make network requests and does not use cloud synchronization. Choosing Support or Privacy Policy from the Help menu asks macOS to open the corresponding public webpage in your default browser.

Children’s privacy

Because Bonsai Ma does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact method on the Bonsai Ma support page.
